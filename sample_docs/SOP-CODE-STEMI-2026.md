# SOP-CODE-STEMI-2026 — Code STEMI (ST-Elevation Myocardial Infarction)

**Domain:** Emergency Medicine / Cardiology
**Trigger:** Severe chest pain, shortness of breath, or diaphoresis.

## Critical Timeline Goals
- Door-to-ECG: obtain and interpret a 12-lead ECG within 10 minutes of arrival.
- Cath Lab Activation: if the ECG confirms ST elevation, bypass the ED if possible and
  directly activate the Cardiac Catheterization Laboratory (CCL).
- Door-to-Balloon: < 90 minutes.

## Immediate Medical Interventions
- Administer 160–325 mg chewed Aspirin immediately.
- Provide sublingual Nitroglycerin (0.3–0.4 mg) every 5 minutes if systolic BP > 100 mmHg.

## Contraindications
Avoid Nitroglycerin if a right ventricular infarction is suspected or if the patient
recently used phosphodiesterase inhibitors (e.g., sildenafil / Viagra).
