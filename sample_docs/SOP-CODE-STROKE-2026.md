# SOP-CODE-STROKE-2026 — Code Stroke (Acute Ischemic Stroke Pathway)

**Domain:** Emergency Medicine / Neurology
**Trigger:** Sudden facial droop, arm drift, or slurred speech (F.A.S.T. criteria).

## Critical Timeline Goals
- Door-to-Doctor: < 10 minutes.
- Door-to-CT Scan: < 25 minutes (non-contrast CT of the head to rule out hemorrhage).
- Last Known Well (LKW): establish the exact time the patient was last at their neurological baseline.

## Intervention Pathway
If the CT shows no bleeding and the LKW is less than 4.5 hours, the patient may be a
candidate for intravenous fibrinolytic therapy (e.g., Alteplase / tPA). The Alteplase
administration window is within 4.5 hours of last known well.

## Contraindications
Do not administer fibrinolytics if blood pressure is actively elevated, there is a
history of intracranial hemorrhage, or blood glucose is < 50 mg/dL.
